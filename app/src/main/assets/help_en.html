Free, simple, and fast RSS news aggregator. No account needed. Tailor your reading to your liking. Backup and share your feeds. Create thematic news sets.

MAIN SCREEN

Add source

Type the main website address in, and tap Find feed. (Enter site.com, and the app will automatically extract its feed channel, e.g. site.com/feed). Then add found feed channels to your sources set.

Viewing modes button: Flow or Group by source

Flow mode displays all the items as they come from all publishers, sorted by time. In Group by source mode you will see them grouped alphabetically by publisher.

Time depth

Choose from how many days in the past you'd like to fetch your feeds. Or Since last visit.

Swiping: Show more or Add bookmark

By default you will only see 3 items per publisher (changeable in Menu: All sources).

  • Swipe left: See all items by a publisher within the selected time depth.
  • Swipe right: Save an item to Bookmarks if you find it important.

Tags

At the bottom of the screen you will find five tags. Tapping any of them will show sources marked by this tag. That way you can create your own thematic digests. See more in Menu: All sources and Menu: Tags.

MENU

All sources

Here you can display all the feed sources you added.

  • Swipe left: Completely delete a source with all its bookmarks and history.
  • Swipe right: Open Limits / Tags window.
    • Limits: Why this is important: some sources (like big media) generate tens or even hundreds items daily, and completely dominate your feed. This option lets you limit their activity to only a few items (from 1 to 20) on main screen.
      To see more items by a publisher, just tap a source from All sources list. You will see all items by that publisher on main screen. (Then hit Back to flow to return to normal view). You can also swipe left on main screen to see more item by a publisher.
    • Tags: Add from 0 to 5 tags to a source to create thematic news digests.

In this window you can also change the source's name.

Bookmarks

Here you will see all bookmarked items. Swipe left to delete.

Export feeds

A very useful option that lets you export all your feeds (with tags) and bookmarks to:

  • A JSON file (e.g. as a backup on your device).
  • The text server dpaste.com (e.g. to share your feeds with a remote friend or open them in Coffee Reader installed on another device). Tap the Export button to generate a link valid for 24 hours. A QR code and sharing options will also be provided.

Import feeds

Type or paste the dpaste.com export link here, scan the QR code, or choose a JSON file with feeds. Only new sources will be imported; existing ones will be omitted.

Tags

Here you can rename tags and toggle their view on/off on the main screen.

Warning: If you rename your tags, they will be ignored after exporting them to a device with another tags structure.
